⚡ How to Play SQL Challenge

🗄️

1. Select a Schema

Choose one of your uploaded ER diagram schemas. The challenge will be based on its tables and columns.

🎯

2. Pick a Difficulty

Choose how hard your challenge will be. Once the challenge starts, difficulty is locked until you finish.

Easy — 3 min, simple SELECT Medium — 2 min, JOINs & GROUP BY Hard — 90 sec, subqueries & aggregation
⌨️

3. Write Your SQL

Read the prompt carefully and write a SQL query that answers it. Use exact table and column names from your schema.

💡

4. You Get 3 Tries

If your first answer is wrong, you'll get a hint and can try again — but each hint costs −10 pts from your score.

🏆

5. Scoring

Base score is graded by AI (0–100).
−10 pts per hint used (max 2 hints).
−15 pts max if time runs out.
+5 pts bonus for correct Hard answers.
Total penalty is capped at 25 pts.
